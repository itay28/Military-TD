Rendered sprites of two models, set together in one scene:

"AGS 40 Balkan" by SpinoCactus (the launcher and its tripod, a stand-in for the AGS-30)
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/85261dcf1d4e43b2b6b7db1818c7c3ae
"Tactical Soldier (game ready) low poly" by MissingTray1151 (the gunner and the assistant gunner)
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/2bfce585144d4403a3ddf76a0e65767a

The PNGs in this folder are top-down renders of those models made by
scripts/render-sprite.py (changed: rendered from above, lit, desaturated, combined, the soldier posed crouched, tinted olive and duplicated, set in a generated sandbag pit, split into hull and turret layers).
russia-grenade-launcher-turntable.webp is a turntable of the same scene (the whole model, 24 views orbiting
it from 30 degrees up, for the Field manual), made by scripts/render-sprite.py --turntable.
The models themselves aren't committed.
