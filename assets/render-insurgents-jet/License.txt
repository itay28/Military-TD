The PNG in this folder is a top-down render of that model made by
scripts/render-sprite.py (changed: national markings and numbers, modelled as decals, removed,
rendered from above in flight, raised off the ground so its shadow falls offset, lit, desaturated).
insurgents-jet-turntable.webp is a turntable of the same model (the whole model, 24 views orbiting
it from 30 degrees up, for the Field manual), made by scripts/render-sprite.py --turntable.
The model itself isn't committed. CC0 needs no credit; it's given anyway.
License checked on the model's Sketchfab page, 2026-11-11.
