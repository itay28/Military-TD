The PNG in this folder is a top-down render of that model made by
scripts/render-sprite.py (changed: rendered from above in flight, raised off the ground so its
shadow falls offset, lit, desaturated).
us-jet-turntable.webp is a turntable of the same model (the whole model, 24 views orbiting
it from 30 degrees up, for the Field manual), made by scripts/render-sprite.py --turntable.
The model itself isn't committed.
