The PNGs in this folder are top-down renders of those models made by
scripts/render-sprite.py (changed: rendered from above, lit, desaturated, combined, the launcher made opaque (its export has alpha 0) and painted dark grey, the soldier posed crouched, tinted green and duplicated, set in a generated sandbag pit, split into hull and turret layers).
china-grenade-launcher-turntable.webp is a turntable of the same scene (the whole model, 24 views orbiting
it from 30 degrees up, for the Field manual), made by scripts/render-sprite.py --turntable.
The models themselves aren't committed.
