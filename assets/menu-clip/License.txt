Menu clip (menu-clip.webm, menu-clip.mp4, menu-clip-small.webm, menu-clip-small.mp4, menu-clip-still.webp)
for Hold the Perimeter

Recorded from the game by tests/menu-clip/capture.spec.ts (npm run menu:clip, issue #568): the menu clip scene on
Training Ground, stepped frame by frame, with a muffled battle track mixed from the game's own sounds. It shows and
plays these assets, each under its own license (see CREDITS.md):
- NAIP aerial imagery (Iowa 2023) by the USDA Farm Service Agency (assets/naip-field-road-treeline/), public domain
- The US renders: render-us-rifle-nest, render-us-aa-gun, render-us-cannon, render-us-mortar (CC-BY 4.0)
- The Russian renders: render-russia-infantry, render-russia-jeep, render-russia-helicopter, render-russia-apc,
  render-russia-tank (CC-BY 4.0)
- Weapon sounds (assets/weapon-sounds/) and battlefield ambience (assets/battlefield-ambience/), CC0 1.0
