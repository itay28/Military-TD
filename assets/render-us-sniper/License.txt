The PNGs in this folder are top-down renders of that model made by
scripts/render-sprite.py (changed: rendered from above, lit, desaturated, posed prone, tinted, duplicated, set in a generated sandbag pit).
us-sniper-turntable.webp is a turntable of the same model (the whole model, 24 views orbiting
it from 30 degrees up, for the Field manual), made by scripts/render-sprite.py --turntable.
The model itself isn't committed.
