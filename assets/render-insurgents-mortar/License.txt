The PNGs in this folder are top-down renders of that model made by
scripts/render-sprite.py (changed: rendered from above, lit, desaturated, the 81 mm MO-3 alone (the two 60 mm mortars removed), set in a generated sandbag pit, split into hull and turret layers).
insurgents-mortar-turntable.webp is a turntable of the same model (the whole model, 24 views orbiting
it from 30 degrees up, for the Field manual), made by scripts/render-sprite.py --turntable.
The model itself isn't committed.
License checked on the model's Sketchfab page, 2026-11-02.
